My process.

A workflow that combines creativity, storytelling and technical precision, from the first brief to the final export.

  1. 01

    Discovery & planning

    Understand the brand, audience and brief to set the visual direction and storytelling approach.

  2. 02

    Storyboard & design

    Style frames, typography layouts and motion references agreed before animation begins.

  3. 03

    Motion & editing

    Animation, transitions, typography, colour grading and sound design in After Effects and Premiere Pro.

  4. 04

    Review & refinement

    Timing, pacing, easing and transitions refined through rounds of feedback.

  5. 05

    Final delivery

    Optimised exports for social media, websites, ads and presentations.
